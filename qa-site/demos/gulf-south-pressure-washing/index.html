Questions

Good to know

Will pressure washing damage my siding or roof?

We soft wash houses and roofs at low pressure with cleaners that kill mildew at the root, so nothing gets blasted or stripped.

Do I need to be home?

No. We just need access to an outdoor water spigot. We send photos when the job is done.

Is it safe for my plants and pets?

We pre-wet and rinse plants and keep pets inside until surfaces are dry.

How long does it last?

Most homes on the Coast stay clean 12 to 18 months. Roofs last longer.
