Questions

Good to know

How soon can I drive on a new driveway?

Light foot traffic after a day, cars after about seven days, heavy trucks after 28.

Do you pull permits?

Yes, when the city or county requires one. It is included in your quote.

Do you use rebar or wire mesh?

Rebar on a grid in every driveway and slab. It costs a little more and lasts a lot longer.

What areas do you cover?

Lincoln, Pike, Copiah, Lawrence and Amite counties.
