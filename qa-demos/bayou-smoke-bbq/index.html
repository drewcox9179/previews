Questions

Good to know

How far ahead should I book catering?

Two to four weeks for most events. Weddings in spring and fall book up months ahead.

Do you do pickup orders?

Yes. Whole briskets, racks and pans of sides with 48 hours notice.

Can the truck come to our event?

Yes, for 75 guests or more within 40 miles of Laurel.

Do you cater outside Jones County?

Yes, across the Pine Belt. A delivery fee applies past 30 miles.
