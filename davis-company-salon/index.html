Before You Book

Booking & Cancellation Policy

We know life happens, and we're always happy to work with you. We just ask for a little heads-up so we can care for all of our guests.
  • 24 hrsNotice

    Cancelling or rescheduling

    Please give us at least 24 hours' notice for any cancellation or schedule change.

  • 50%Of service

    Late cancellations

    Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your appointment are charged 50% of the scheduled service fee.

  • 100%Of service

    No-shows

    Missed appointments without notice are charged 100% of the scheduled service fee.

  • 50%Deposit

    Recurring no-shows

    Guests with more than one no-show will be asked to place a 50% service deposit to secure future appointments.
